# Phase 1 Summary

RetroInfer and full attention share the same model and decode codepath; the only difference is the active KV cache implementation selected by `_activate_cache`. Full attention keeps all KV entries live and uses `flash_attn_with_kvcache` over the whole prefix plus generated context. RetroInfer instead partitions context into a `steady_zone` that is always kept exactly and a long-range region that is compressed with segmented k-means into centroid, value-sum, mask, and cluster-size metadata. During decode it scores the current query against centroids, selects only the top `nprobe` clusters plus an optional estimation zone, gathers the corresponding pages into an execution buffer, and attends over `steady_zone + retrieved pages`, optionally merging in an estimation-zone summary.

What is lost at budget2 is exact access to all historical KV pages outside the steady zone. Non-selected clusters are omitted entirely from the decode computation, and even selected estimation-zone content can be represented only through centroid/value-sum summaries rather than full token-level KV. So the approximation error is structural: some evidence is dropped, and some evidence is coarsened.

Early verify acceptance means the verifier’s argmax agrees with the proposed token sequence for the accepted prefix. It does not guarantee similar distribution shape, similar hidden state, or robustness under small retrieval changes. Final verify rejection means the first position where full attention is evaluated over the same pending span produces a different authoritative token. Mechanistically, that indicates the exact KV evidence excluded or distorted by RetroInfer changed the ranking at that position enough to flip the argmax.
